What is the best dictation app for Mac with a keyboard shortcut that works in every app? +

AngelWrite, featured by iTopnews.de as an app insider tip, is a push-to-talk dictation app for macOS. Hold a key, speak, release. Your text appears right where your cursor is: in Mail, Slack, Word, Notion, or any browser. No app switching, no clicking.

Can I read along live while dictating? +

Yes. A floating preview window shows the recognized text word by word while you speak, including the active mode and shortcut in the window header. You read along at all times and stay in control. The preview is read-only; the inserted text is finalized as usual. On by default since version 1.2.0.

Do I have to hold the key the whole time while dictating? +

No — hold and release is the default; alternatively it works hands-free: tap once to start recording, tap again to stop, Esc cancels.

Can I add my own technical terms and proper names? +

Yes. Use the word list to add names, brands, and technical terms; AngelWrite spells them correctly. Local, no audio, in all plans (including Local).

Is AngelWrite GDPR-compliant? Are my voice recordings sent to the cloud? +

In local mode no audio leaves your device — fully offline, ideal for lawyers, doctors, and consultants. In Pro mode audio is transferred encrypted to our server and OpenAI Whisper. GDPR-compliant in both modes. Details in the privacy policy.

What is the best replacement for Dragon Dictate on the Mac? +

AngelWrite is a modern alternative to Dragon Dictate for macOS. Dragon for Mac was discontinued. AngelWrite takes over: local AI transcription based on OpenAI Whisper, one-time purchase without a subscription. After a one-time download of the local model, transcription works without internet.

How is AngelWrite different from Apple's built-in dictation? +

Apple dictation stops after 30–60 seconds, recognizes German worse than English, and doesn't improve your text. AngelWrite has no time limit, uses Whisper, refines text with AI modes, and works via keyboard shortcut in every app — no Siri, no cloud required. Both can run in parallel: during setup AngelWrite helps you pick a shortcut that doesn't collide with macOS dictation.

Does AngelWrite conflict with Apple/Fn dictation on the Mac? +

No. During setup AngelWrite detects whether your shortcut collides with macOS dictation, tests it with you, and suggests a free shortcut if needed. You can keep Apple dictation in parallel.

Does AngelWrite work offline — on a train, abroad, without Wi-Fi? +

In local mode (Original), AngelWrite runs offline — after a one-time download of the local model, transcription happens entirely on your Mac, without a server connection. The 7-day Pro trial and Pro mode use cloud transcription and AI processing and need an internet connection for that.

How much faster am I when I dictate instead of typing? +

Speaking is 3× faster than typing. Average typing speed: 53 WPM. Speaking: 161 WPM (Stanford HCI Lab). The savings calculator on this page shows what that means for your workload.

What is the difference between Local, Pro, and Advanced? +

Local (€19 one-time): offline, maximum privacy, app updates & bug fixes included. Pro (€99 / year): all AI modes, cloud transcription, new features. Advanced (€149 one-time): your own API key, your own prompts, unlimited requests.

Which professions benefit most from AngelWrite? +

Lawyers, doctors, consultants, authors, journalists, executives, and developers: anyone who writes a lot every day and wants to communicate faster and more clearly.

What are AngelWrite's system requirements? Which Macs does it run on? +

AngelWrite runs on macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer. A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) is recommended: local speech recognition is especially fast thanks to the Neural Engine. Intel Macs are supported as well. Three one-time system permissions are needed (microphone, accessibility, input monitoring) — the app guides you through setup. "Input monitoring" sounds stricter than it is: AngelWrite only needs it to detect when you hold your dictation keys (e.g. fn + ⇧). It never reads your texts or keystrokes — only the hold of the recording keys is processed. Not sure if your Mac qualifies? Try AngelWrite free for 7 days and test it on your machine.

Can AngelWrite rewrite, translate, or shorten selected text? +

Yes — that's Rewrite by voice: select text in any app, hold your key, and say what should change ("in German", "shorter", "more formal", "explain this in simple terms"). The result sits on your clipboard; paste it with ⌘V wherever you want. Rewrite is included in Pro & Advanced.

Where does selected text go when rewriting? +

The same way as your dictation. If cloud mode is active, the selected text is sent encrypted to the AngelWrite cloud and is not stored there. If local mode is active, it never leaves your Mac. The result lands on your clipboard — pasting is up to you.

Can I use AngelWrite fully locally with my own vocabulary? +

Yes. In the Local plan, transcription runs entirely on your Mac — no audio leaves the device. You add your own vocabulary (names, brands, technical terms) to the word list, also locally and without audio, one by one or via CSV import. On first launch AngelWrite downloads a compact speech-recognition model once; after that, local mode works offline.
